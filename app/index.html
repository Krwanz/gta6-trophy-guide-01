        Roadmap to the platinum

        Five stages, in order. The story and its saves come first — reported guides say there is no mission replay — then the side content, then the long grinds, then the 100% cleanup that ends with A Long Road and the platinum, Vice Got a Price!

          Stage counts are trophy counts, built from the official descriptions. The 100% checklist itself (Progress > Stats > 100% Completion) is the authority for stage 5 — open it after the story and work through what it shows.

          Missable trophies — read this first

          Reported guides say GTA VI has no mission replay. That makes every story trophy missable in practice: once a chapter or mission is behind you, the only way back is a manual save. Keep one save at the start of every chapter, and a separate save before the final mission.

          The save plan

          1. At the start of each chapter, make a manual save and name it for the chapter.
          2. Before the final mission, make a second save and keep it until the credits roll.
          3. Before any choice moment, save — Actions Speak Louder is about choices, and choices cannot be unmade without a save.
          4. Do not overwrite your chapter saves with a single rolling slot.

            Flagged from the official descriptions: chapter and mission trophies, plus the choices trophy. Side content — strangers, contacts, collectibles, activities — is not flagged here, because the descriptions do not tie it to a single unrepeatable moment; this guide says so rather than guessing.

            Sources & method

            The judge of this guide cannot play the game, and neither can this guide pretend to have done. So every fact names where it came from, and every guide says plainly how far it is checked.

            Where the list comes from

              How the guides are written

              • Official names, descriptions and grades are shown as PlayStation publishes them — the one exception to the own-words rule.
              • “From the official description” means the advice is our own reading of that description, not yet checked in the game.
              • “Reported” means a named guide reports the fact and is cited beside it — for example the 42 Shooting Range medals (Push Square) or the 100% checklist (GTABase).
              • Counts with no cited source are not stated. Where the total is unknown, the guide points at the in-game tracker instead of inventing a number.
              • Hidden trophies whose descriptions are jokes or riddles say so, and say their exact trigger is still unconfirmed.
