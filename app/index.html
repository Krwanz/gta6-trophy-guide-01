        Roadmap to the platinum

        Three stages, in order, from PowerPyx's roadmap: the story (60–80 hours), then the 100% cleanup (about 100 hours), then the stranger strands and the last one-offs (about 10 hours). One playthrough is enough, and the whole run is about 200 hours. The 100% checklist itself (Progress > Stats > 100% Completion) is the authority for stage 2 — open it after the story and work through what it shows.

          Missable trophies — read this first

          Only one trophy may be missable: Positive Mental Attitude. PowerPyx flags it; Push Square lists no known missables. Every story trophy unlocks automatically and cannot be missed — that is what the launch guides say, and it is why this guide no longer flags the chapter and mission trophies.

          The save plan

          1. The game has 15 manual save slots and 1 autosave, and no mission replay. Keep a save at the start and end of each chapter (10 saves), then rotate the remaining 5 during free-roam cleanup.
          2. In Chapter 1, do Lucia's balcony meditation at Jason's house — after 'Down to Earth', talk to her in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and accept. It is gone after Chapter 1, and it is still unclear whether the trophy needs it.
          3. Before any choice moment, save — but note that Actions Speak Louder is about 500 small profile actions, not one big choice, and it comes naturally.
          4. Do not overwrite your chapter saves with a single rolling slot.

            Looks missable, but is not

            PowerPyx lists things that look missable and do not affect any trophy or the 100% checklist: many optional missions disappear after a while (there are far more than the 100 you need); some quest givers, Kenton for one, go away for part of the story and come back; some fences leave for good, but none of them has a trophy quest; a few magazine collectibles sit in story buildings that close off, but they also spawn elsewhere; quests with an hourglass icon can fail on a timer, but none is tied to a trophy; and the Jason–Lucia relationship does not matter for trophies. One warning that is not about trophies: the Irredeemable criminal profile is permanent and makes the police more aggressive.

            Flagged from the launch guides (PowerPyx, Push Square), not from the descriptions alone: the chapter and mission trophies were flagged here in the first version of this guide, and the launch guides proved that wrong — all 12 story trophies are automatic.

            Sources & method

            The game launched on 19 November 2026, and the launch guides were published the same week — so this version is built from what PowerPyx, Push Square and GTABase confirmed in play, not from the pre-launch list alone. Every fact names where it came from, and every guide says plainly how far it is checked.

            Where the list comes from

              How the guides are written

              • Official names, descriptions and grades are shown as PlayStation publishes them — the one exception to the own-words rule.
              • “Reported” means a launch guide confirms the method and is cited beside it — PowerPyx's trophy guide and roadmap (18 Nov 2026), Push Square's guide, or GTABase. 52 of the 55 guides are reported.
              • “Unconfirmed” means PowerPyx lists the trigger as currently unknown: In The Beginning…, Bad Luck, I Guess and Ten Pin. Those three guides state no method rather than inventing one.
              • “From the official description” would mean the advice is our own reading of that description, not yet checked in the game — no guide in this version needs that label.
              • Difficulty (1–5) and time are this guide's own estimates for each trophy, built from PowerPyx's roadmap (story 60–80 hours, 100% cleanup about 100 hours, strands about 10 hours). The set as a whole is 4/10 and about 200 hours (PowerPyx; Push Square says 250+).
              • Hidden trophies: 28 of the 55 are hidden on PlayStation until earned. The spoiler setting hides all 28 until earned; Push Square's guide marks 27 (it does not mark Personal Best hidden).
              • Cheats disable trophies and stop the game saving (PowerPyx and Push Square). Keep them off the save file you use for the platinum.
              • Hidden trophies whose descriptions are jokes or riddles say so, and say their exact trigger is still unconfirmed where that is the case.
